Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Hub is a personal desktop tool used by one person on their own computer. It is not sold and not available for download. This page explains what Hub does with Google user data.

What Hub reads from Google

  • Read your email (Gmail, read-only): Hub reads recent messages in the inbox and their labels (sender, subject, date and text) so they can be shown and sorted inside Hub. Hub cannot delete, change or label mail.
  • Send email on your behalf: Hub sends a reply only when the user taps Send on that reply. It never sends on its own.
  • Hub also reads the Google account's email address, to show which mailbox is connected.

Where the data stays, and for how long

  • Copies of the mail are kept in Hub's folder on the user's own computer: at most the newest 500 messages per mailbox. Hub deletes older copies.
  • The copies are also sent to the user's own phone when it is paired with Hub on their home network.
  • The Google sign-in key is locked with Windows' own protection on that computer. Hub never copies it to the phone, its backups or its logs.
  • Hub has no online server, no ads and no tracking or analytics. The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for advertising.

The AI assistant

Hub has an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic). Nothing from a mailbox goes to the assistant until the user allows it for that mailbox. Hub asks once, and the answer is no until the user taps Allow.

For an allowed mailbox, the assistant gets mail text in these cases:

  • When Hub's rules cannot sort a new message, its sender, subject and a short snippet are sent once, so the assistant can suggest where it belongs.
  • When the user asks Hub to draft a reply, that message's text is sent so the assistant can write the draft. The user checks the draft before anything is sent.
  • When the user taps "Ask about this" on a message, its sender and subject go to the assistant with the question.
  • When the user asks the assistant a question, it may read the stored mail copies of allowed mailboxes to answer it.

For any mailbox:

  • When the user taps "Send to team" on a message, the whole message (sender, subject and text) goes to one of the user's team chats, which Anthropic stores. Hub sends mail text to a team chat only on this tap.
  • The assistant can never send mail. Only the user's tap on Send does that.
  • In Hub's settings (Privacy) the user can turn the assistant's access off for one mailbox or for all mail at any time.
  • Anthropic does not use this text to train its models, and keeps it for up to 30 days (longer only if Anthropic's safety systems flag it).

Google's rules

Hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The user can disconnect the mailbox in Hub's settings, which deletes the sign-in key. They can also remove Hub's access at any time in their Google Account under Security, "Third-party apps with account access". Mail copies are deleted by removing Hub's folder, and the Hub app's data on the phone (or by uninstalling it there).
